Step 01

Executive Snapshot

An institutional summary of Q4FY26's operating reality — separating the headline noise from the underlying signal across India's most complex multi-segment consumer platform.

Quarter Assessment
Fundamentally Strong with Critical Structural Milestone. Q4FY26 marks the quarter Blinkit crossed EBITDA breakeven — a milestone the market has waited for. Food delivery growth is re-accelerating for the third consecutive quarter, approaching the long-guided 20%+ YoY threshold. District's losses are narrowing meaningfully. The quarter was seasonally weak for quick commerce (Q4 is structurally the weakest), and the apparent 186% revenue growth is wholly optical due to the 1P accounting transition. On a like-for-like basis, the business grew 64% YoY — still exceptional, though off a nascent base. The primary concern remains: treasury income of ₹319 crore equals 74% of total Adjusted EBITDA, meaning the core operating profit engine is thinner than headlines suggest.

What the Market May Be Missing

The treasury income dependency is frequently overlooked. At ₹319 crore vs. Adj. EBITDA of ₹429 crore, 74% of reported EBITDA comes from parking ₹18,000 crore in debt securities. Strip it out and the underlying business EBITDA is ~₹110 crore — still positive, but a very different picture for operating leverage math.

The 1P accounting shift in Blinkit means YoY revenue comparisons are nearly meaningless. Investors anchoring on the 186% headline figure are comparing apples to oranges. The genuine LFL revenue growth was 64% — exceptional, but not 186%.

Blinkit's mature market economics (Delhi NCR at 5–6% margin) prove the unit economics thesis conclusively. The market is pricing in execution risk across 2,243 stores — the mature-market data should resolve this discount over time.

What Matters for Long-Term Investors

The singular question is whether Blinkit can replicate Delhi NCR economics across 3,000+ stores. If yes, this business generates ₹4,000–5,000 crore in annual EBITDA at scale. If not, the current valuation is deeply optimistic.

Food delivery's proven margin stability at 5.5% despite NAOV compression is genuinely impressive. Management is demonstrating that platform fees and cost efficiency can offset lower average orders — a structural shift, not a temporary one.

The physical infrastructure moat — 17mn sq ft of dark store space, trained delivery partner networks, cold chain logistics — is far harder to replicate than algorithms. Competitors need capital and time. Eternal is 18 months ahead on store count and operational depth.

Management's $1 billion EBITDA by FY29 target implies roughly 12x the current quarterly run-rate in three years. The path is visible but demands near-perfect execution across all four business lines simultaneously.

Revenue Quality Assessment

Quick Commerce (Optical Distortion — HIGH). The 674% YoY Adj. Revenue growth in QC is entirely due to the 1P model shift. On LFL basis, QC grew 126% YoY — still the strongest growth engine in the portfolio. The accounting change inflated the consolidated revenue base dramatically but did not create value; inventory ownership shifts COGS into cost of goods sold, which is reflected in the Gross Profit line (26.9% of NOV in Q4).

Food Delivery (Genuine Growth — HIGH QUALITY). Revenue growing 29.7% YoY vs. NOV growing 18.8% indicates improving monetisation per order — platform fees and Gold subscriptions are yielding higher take rates even as NAOV declines. Contribution at 10.2% of NOV remained remarkably stable, proving unit economics are intact despite the push into budget-conscious segments.

Hyperpure (Structural Transition). Reported revenue declined 46.8% YoY due to the shift of restaurant-facing inventory into the 1P Blinkit model. LFL growth was +37% — a genuine acceleration from +33% in Q3. The business has now turned EBITDA positive at 0.5% margin.

Margin & Earnings Quality

EBITDA Quality — Moderate. The ₹429 crore Adj. EBITDA includes ₹319 crore in treasury income (cash received from ₹18,000 crore corpus in bonds and mutual funds). The core operating EBITDA — stripping treasury — is approximately ₹110 crore. This is growing, positive, and real, but investors must understand the structural dependency on financial income that will compress as the corpus deploys into dark store capex over the next 24 months.

ESOP Expense — Meaningful Dilution. FY26 ESOP expense was ₹819 crore, against which ₹208 crore was recorded in Q4 alone. This is added back to arrive at Adjusted EBITDA but represents real economic cost to shareholders through dilution. At ~₹819 crore annually against ~₹1,716 crore annualized Adj. EBITDA, ESOP represents a 47% drag on true economic earnings.

PAT Quality — Improving. PAT of ₹174 crore in Q4FY26 (FY26 total: ₹366 crore) is the first full-year profit in the company's history. However, total comprehensive income was negative (₹-166 crore for FY26) due to MTM losses of ₹532 crore on debt instruments in the investment portfolio — bond price decline from uncertain macro environment. These are unrealized and will reverse upon maturity.

Working Capital Forensics

Inventory Explosion (+11.9x YoY). Inventories jumped from ₹176 crore (March 2025) to ₹2,181 crore (March 2026) — entirely due to the 1P model transition. This is expected and disclosed. Critically, management states inventory days are "fairly steady" and NWC days are within the guided ~18-day range. No evidence of inventory manipulation or channel stuffing.

Trade Payables Growing Proportionally. Trade payables rose from ₹1,536 crore to ₹3,010 crore — broadly in line with the inventory ownership shift. The payable extension is structural (buying from brands) rather than distress-driven. Supplier relationships appear stable.

NWC Improved QoQ in Q4. NWC decreased by ₹55 crore QoQ — positive signal — primarily from settling with sellers post-1P transition. This resolved a temporary cash lockup from Q3's transition costs.

Lease Liabilities: The Hidden Balance Sheet. Lease liabilities of ₹4,592 crore (up from ₹2,045 crore March 2025) represent the rapid dark store expansion. This is structural investment, not hidden debt — but it materially increases economic obligations. ROU assets match at ₹4,294 crore. Net lease obligation is manageable given the ₹18,000 crore cash corpus.

Financial Architecture Verdict
The balance sheet is a fortress. ₹18,000 crore in cash and investments dwarfs all liabilities (excluding leases). Q4FY27 could see meaningfully lower reported EBITDA if treasury income normalises faster than business EBITDA grows.

Share capital increased from ₹907 crore to ₹919 crore (1.3%) even in one year. Deepinder's returned unvested shares help somewhat, but ongoing grants make true EPS calculation misleading vs. Adj. EBITDA metrics.

Outstanding demand orders and SCNs from GST authorities regarding delivery charges: ₹420 crore (Oct 2019–Mar 2022), ₹8 crore (AP FY23), ₹13 crore (Gujarat FY23), ₹6 crore (AP FY24). Company contests all claims and has expert legal opinion on merits. Auditor (Deloitte) flagged as Emphasis of Matter but did not modify opinion. W.e.f. OCI Loss of ₹532 Crore — Bond MTM

Low Customer Acquisition Cost Window
Management noted on the Q4 call that competitor pull-back on marketing has created an environment of "extremely low cost of customer acquisition." This CAC compression, if sustained through Q1FY27, could create a cohort of customers with above-average LTV.
④
District Loss Curve Inflection
From -₹121 crore (Q3) to -₹81 crore (Q4) in one quarter — a 33% sequential improvement in absolute losses. If the 4–6 quarter breakeven guidance holds, District could turn EBITDA positive by Q2FY28, removing a meaningful consolidated drag and unlocking positive operating leverage from a third business line simultaneously.
⑤
AI as a Market Expansion Engine
Zomato's Healthy Mode and Natural Language Search (NLS) are early AI features that could structurally expand the addressable customer base — specifically older users, non-native language speakers, and Tier 2/3 consumers who struggle with static app flows. If even 5% of previously inaccessible users onboard through AI-assisted discovery, that is a materially larger TAM at near-zero marginal cost.
⑥
Hyperpure EBITDA Positive — Opens Path to ₹500+ Crore Business
The farm-to-fork B2B supply chain serving 400k+ restaurants has finally turned cash-generative. LFL growth accelerating to 37% vs. 33% in Q3. Hyperpure could become a meaningful contributor (~₹2,000+ crore in revenue, ₹200–300 crore EBITDA) within 3 years — not currently priced in by most models.

What Growth is Priced In

At ₹241/share with ₹2,32,747 crore market cap, the stock is pricing in the full realisation of the $1 billion EBITDA vision by FY28–29, sustained QC growth at 60%+ CAGR, food delivery at 20%+ YoY, and District reaching profitability — essentially a perfect execution scenario across all four business lines simultaneously.

Valuation Quality Assessment

ROCE Quality — Currently Weak (2.97%). This is unsurprising for a business still investing aggressively. The ROCE trajectory is what matters: Delhi NCR stores generate 40%+ ROCE at maturity. If aggregate ROCE reaches 20%+ by FY28 across 3,000+ stores, the valuation becomes justified on return-on-capital grounds.

FCF Quality — Still Negative. FCF of -₹1,119 crore (FY26) reflects the dark store capex cycle. Moat Durability — HIGH. Physical infrastructure moats in India are the highest-durability competitive advantages possible. The combination of brand equity (Zomato, Blinkit, District as distinct mental triggers) and physical scale (dark stores, delivery networks, supply chains) creates a compounding moat that improves with scale. This justifies premium multiple over a pure tech play.

Balance Sheet Quality — EXCEPTIONAL. ₹18,000 crore in cash at current stock price provides approximately 8% of market cap as pure liquid value. This backstop and the ability to fund growth internally without equity dilution is genuinely premium-worthy in the Indian market context.

Institutional Conclusion on Valuation
Eternal is not a mediocre business at a premium valuation — it is a genuinely exceptional compounding machine at a price that leaves limited near-term margin of safety but significant long-term upside. The 636x trailing P/E is irrelevant; the business was barely profitable a year ago. The correct lens is: does the market cap of ₹2.33 lakh crore reflect a reasonable present value of the ₹8,500 crore EBITDA business that management credibly targets for FY29? At a 30x forward EBITDA on FY29 estimates, the answer is yes — but only if you have a 3-year minimum holding horizon and believe in execution quality. For investors with shorter horizons or lower risk tolerance, the premium is not compensated. The stock trades at approximately fair value for FY29 outcomes — there is no deep value, but there is also no dangerous overvaluation given the quality of the underlying assets.

Industry Structure Evolution — 3-Year View

Quick Commerce consolidation is inevitable. Three well-capitalised players (Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart, Zepto) cannot all survive at current burn rates. Either rational behaviour emerges within 12–18 months or one player secures a structural capital advantage. Blinkit's existing dark store infrastructure (2,243 locations) is the most defensible position because it cannot be replicated cheaply or quickly.

Food delivery TAM expansion is the under-appreciated story. India has 140 crore people; only 25 million order food online monthly. The budget segment push (₹99 MOV, sub-₹250 meals) is an attempt to reach the next 100 million. If even 20% of the "latent demand" market converts, Zomato's MTU could double to 50mn in 3 years without displacing a single existing customer.

AI will not disrupt Eternal near-term — but it will enhance it. The Founder's Letter's Google analogy is analytically correct: general-purpose interfaces fail at high-frequency habitual commerce. But Eternal's own AI deployment (NLS, Healthy Mode, route optimisation) will create compounding advantages in customer acquisition cost and retention in Tier 2/3 markets over a 3–5 year horizon.
